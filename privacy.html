Privacy Policy

Productivity Assistant is a Chrome extension designed to help users manage focus sessions, break timers, restricted sites, and local productivity insights.

What data we collect

We collect only the data needed for the extension to function locally in your browser. This may include:

  • Timer preferences and break messages you configure
  • Blocked-site settings and focus mode preferences
  • Domain-level usage data such as site names, time spent, and visit counts
  • Optional AI settings you enable

What we do not collect

We do not collect or transmit personal identifiers, passwords, full browsing history, page content, or sensitive information. We do not collect your email address, name, location, or payment information.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the extension's local features, such as:

  • Scheduling break reminders
  • Showing focus-mode or restriction overlays
  • Displaying local usage insights

Local storage

All settings and usage data are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's local storage API. We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for unrelated purposes.

Optional AI features

If you enable optional AI features, the extension may send only limited, aggregated information to the AI provider you choose in order to generate insights or reminder text. It does not send full browsing history or raw page content unless you explicitly choose to share that information through your AI provider.

Third-party services

Optional AI features may use third-party AI providers such as OpenAI or Azure OpenAI. Any such use is governed by the privacy terms of the provider you select.

Your controls

You can export or remove your data at any time from the extension's settings UI, or by removing the extension from your browser.

Contact

If you have privacy questions or concerns, please contact the extension developer through the support channel listed in the extension listing.
